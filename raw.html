        How it works
        
Four steps, all on your phone

      

      

        
01
The key is split
This page holds most of a Zcash viewing key. The shirt holds the last 16 characters. Together they make the whole key.

        
02
It can read, never spend
A viewing key can read what a wallet received. It can never spend. The wearer chose to share it with you. That's privacy: not secrecy, but choosing who sees what.

        
03
How the message unlocks
Your 16 characters complete the key, and its checksum confirms them (one typo gets fixed). The key finds the one note sent to the shirt's address in this transaction, works out that note's secret, and unscrambles the bytes printed on the shirt into the sentence.

        
04
Why every character counts
The key is scrambled before it's written out, so those 16 characters aren't one particular part of it. The page needs every character of it.
